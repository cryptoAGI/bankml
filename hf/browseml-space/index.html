what this is: three builds, the oracle, the limits

browseML is bankML's engine compiled to WebAssembly. Before it answers, it checks the model as bankml serve does: the GGUF guard, then the sha256 of all 248 MB against its pin. Every answer it gives carries serve's receipt.

buildused when
browseml-mt-relaxed.wasmthreads, relaxed SIMD compiled here and fused on this machine
browseml-mt.wasmthreads, otherwise (Firefox, or not fused)
browseml.wasmone thread: the page is not cross-origin isolated (or Safari)

All three are exact, and they give the same answers. On llama-server b11192's nine recorded conversations, every text, finish reason and token count is identical: browseml.wasm 9 of 9 and browseml-mt-relaxed.wasm on 2 threads 9 of 9.

Limits: threads need cross-origin isolation, so Safari runs on one thread. The first download is 248 MB. A tab holds about twice the model while it loads. An answer cannot be stopped midway.
